# MAFI 2026

The complete, conflict-free MAFI brand pack as a plugin. It works in Claude on the web, the desktop app (Chat and Cowork), the mobile apps and Claude Code. It gives Claude everything about **MAFI — for Agricultural Produce Industries**: the company, all 26 products, the voice, the visual identity, the creative playbook, every approved asset, and past work for reference.

**Install it together with "MAFI 2026 – IQF Photos".** That companion plugin holds the 12 IQF top-view photos in 4K. They're kept separate because Claude allows at most 200 MB per plugin.

## Components
| Component | Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Skills | 1 | `mafi-2026`: the full brand pack. It loads automatically for any MAFI design, copy, product or brand question. |
| Agents, hooks, MCP servers | 0 | Not needed |

## What's inside (complete and unchanged, full resolution)
- `README.md` (start here)
- `01_Company.md` through `07_Sources_and_Decisions.md`
- `brand-board.png`
- `data/`: `products.json` and `company.json`
- `tokens/`: CSS and JSON
- `assets/`:
  - logos and the leaf and sprout marks
  - glass-bowl photos for all 26 products
  - 4K top-view photos for Citrus, Tomato & Multi-Fruits and Freeze Dry
  - lineups, imagery and film stills
- `reference/`: posts, brochure, profile, identity manual, panels, deck, contact sheets

## Install
In Claude on the web or in the desktop app, go to **Customize > Plugins > Add > Upload plugin**, then choose `mafi-2026.plugin`. Repeat for `mafi-2026-iqf-photos.plugin`. Both plugins then follow your Claude account to chat, Cowork and Claude Code. You need a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise).

## Usage
Ask for anything MAFI-related, for example:
- "Design a MAFI leaflet for IQF strawberry"
- "Write a LinkedIn post for MAFI at SIAL Paris"
- "What's the shelf life of MAFI's mango purée?"
