# 01 · Company

## In one paragraph
**MAFI — for Agricultural Produce Industries** (Arabic: **لتصنيع الحاصلات الزراعية**) is a joint **Egyptian–Emirati** venture making **premium B2B agri-food ingredients**: citrus concentrates and juices, tomato and multi-fruit concentrates and purées, IQF (individually quick frozen) fruits and vegetables, and freeze-dried fruit. Its integrated **157,000 m²** complex in **Sadat City, Egypt** brings together **five specialised factories** and **23 production and packaging lines**, with a planned annual production capacity of **more than 100,000 tonnes**. It is **100% export-oriented**, serving food manufacturers in North America, Europe, Asia and the GCC.

**Tagline:** *Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation*

## The complex
**Location:** El-Sewedy Industrial Park, Industria Sadat City, Menoufia, Egypt. It sits close to fertile agricultural land and major ports. The export gateways are **Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, Port Said, Suez and Adabiya**.

**Site plan:**
- Land area: 157,000 m²
- Perimeter fence: 1.8 km
- Main building: about 55,000 m²
- On-site administration / headquarters building: about 4,000 m² built-up area
- Future expansion zones: about 31,200 m² (west) and 3,000 m² (east)
- Two gates
- Zones: raw-material receiving (×2), Concentrate, Cloudy, IQF, Freeze-Dry, and Final Product Dispatch

**Factory signage** reads, for example, "IQF FACTORY", "FREEZE DRIED FACTORY" and "CITRUS CONCENTRATES & CLOUDY FACTORY". It is set in green capitals with the leaf. The main facade carries "FOR AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE INDUSTRIES | MAFI | لتصنيع الحاصلات الزراعية" and the tagline.

**On-site HQ building** (architectural renders):
- Exterior: a wood-fin and dark-glass building with royal palms, reflecting pools and a mirror-steel ring sculpture.
- Inside: a landscaped atrium, a podcasting/broadcast centre, a boardroom and the chairman's office.
- A **VIP visitor corridor** with a viewing window onto the production floor.

**Integrated process:** harvest → receiving laboratories → washing, sorting, preparation → processing and concentration, or IQF, or freeze-drying → aseptic filling or freezing → automated packing → cold storage → reefer dispatch → port.

### Technology & quality
- **Sorting and inspection:** optical sorting (TOMRA), X-ray and metal detection, machine-vision grading.
- **Controls and automation:** PLC and SCADA control, AGVs, automated pallet-shuttle racking, WMS with FIFO, ERP (Oracle), high-speed packaging and palletising.
- **AI-driven operations:** crop planning, predictive quality control and real-time analytics. The motto is *predict, respond, improve*.
- **Hygiene:** each IQF product line has its own dedicated area to prevent cross-contamination. Advanced **CIP** (clean-in-place) systems use food-grade cleaning solutions.
- **Quality and traceability:** a quality-management system with full traceability from raw-material sourcing to final shipment, built on HACCP.

### Utilities & energy
- Five Cochran boilers (60 t/h steam, natural gas) with heat recovery and advanced combustion controls.
- Closed-loop cooling with BAC cooling towers.
- Industrial **ammonia refrigeration** (Grade Refrigeration).
- Water treatment, industrial wastewater treatment, air compressors, gas systems, electrical networks and transformers (Hitachi Energy).
- A planned **6 MW solar** power system, with panels on the main building roof.
- Planned **quad-generation** supplying electricity, steam, hot water and chilled water.

### Cold chain & logistics
- Eight large refrigerated rooms, up to 45,000 pallets / 50,000 tonnes of product.
- Eight outbound reefer container loading gates.
- Reefer containers in MAFI livery.
- Sea freight from Egypt's Mediterranean and Red Sea ports.

## Sourcing & sustainability
**Sourcing model:** three channels: **own production, contract farming and external suppliers**. MAFI partners with growers using water-efficient irrigation, regenerative soil methods and Integrated Pest Management (IPM). A cooperation protocol with Egypt's Ministry of Agriculture and the Agricultural Bank of Egypt supports contract farming.

**Building:** the complex is designed to **LEED Gold** (USGBC). Use "LEED-aligned" when space is short.

**Sustainability commitments** (the full list of 11):
1. **Water Conservation:** smart irrigation and water-saving technology in fields and plants.
2. **Recycling & Repurposing:** by-products become secondary materials, for example orange peel into cloudy concentrate, oil and aroma.
3. **Green Supply Chain:** low-emission logistics.
4. **Sustainable Sourcing:** ethical, environmentally sound growers.
5. **Renewable Energy Integration:** solar and clean energy.
6. **Eco-Friendly Packaging:** recyclable and biodegradable materials.
7. **Advanced Traceability:** farm-to-factory data systems.
8. **Waste Management:** waste reduction and recycling across farms and factories.
9. **Empowering Rural Communities:** infrastructure for clean water and energy.
10. **Social Justice & Human Rights:** ethical labour, and zero tolerance of modern slavery.
11. **Food Security & Nutrition:** locally sourced, nutritious food that preserves Egypt's natural resources.

## Markets & customers
- **Key markets:** North America, Europe, Asia and the GCC. Reach extends to Africa and MENA.
- **Customers:** F&B manufacturers, processors, retailers and private-label brands, HoReCa/foodservice, and sustainability-focused brands.
- **Lead product:** IQF strawberry is the anchor product in buyer conversations. Concentrates, purées, freeze-dried fruit and calibrated vegetables follow.
- **How a buyer qualifies MAFI:** documents → samples → audit → factory visit → trial containers → seasonal volume. Trust is a sales asset, so transparency (factory tours, the visitor corridor, open sample handling) is part of the brand.

## Governance & people
- **Governance:** clear decision rights from the shareholders' general assembly to the CEO. The board and its committees cover risk, audit, nominations and investment. A corporate governance manual (developed with PwC) covers transparency, compliance and related-party transactions.
- **People:** MAFI combines agricultural experts with AI and engineering talent. It recruits through global agencies and universities, and favours local hiring to strengthen rural job markets.
