# 02 · Products

MAFI sells **26 B2B ingredients in four families**. Every product is supplied in bulk industrial packaging (steel drums, aseptic bag-in-drum, IBCs or lined cartons), and several are also available in **retail and private-label formats** (see the end of this file).

Buyers are F&B manufacturers, processors, retailers and private-label brands, HoReCa/foodservice, and sustainability-focused brands. **MAFI does not sell consumer brands.** Even a retail pack is made for someone else's shelf or MAFI's private-label offer.

> Specs are verbatim from the **Products Brochure (Sept 2026)**, the authority for the portfolio. Capacities come from the **corporate film (Sept 2026)**. Each family's colour comes from the brochure. Machine-readable version: `data/products.json`.

Total planned annual production capacity: **more than 100,000 tonnes**. When you need a single headline number, use this one, not the sum of the lines.

**Strapline punctuation:** the family straplines use a **two-dot ellipsis with a space after it** (`Naturally Fresh.. Conveniently Frozen`). This is a deliberate brand habit, so keep it.

**Two product image directions** (`assets/products/`). Every one of the 26 products exists in both:
- `top-view/`: **overhead, white bowl, transparent background**, high resolution (about 3,700 px for most; 1,254 px for tomato concentrate, corn-on-the-cob, green peas and the four freeze-dried cuts). Best for catalogue grids, flat-lays, hero shots and spec sheets.
- `glass-bowls/`: **three-quarter view in clear glass bowls on white**, cut from the official family lineups (about 440–1,000 px). Best for product rows, comparisons and portfolio strips. The full lineups are in `_lineups/`.
- **Freeze-dried strawberry** has all four cuts in both directions: `_whole`, `_sliced`, `_diced`, `_powder` (plus `_mixed` in top view).
- **Use one direction per layout.** Don't mix top view and glass bowls in the same row.

**Family colour coding:** in print, each family owns its colour. It goes on the first word of the product title (the second word stays black or blue), on the spec panels (as the tint), on the seasonality tabs and on the page-edge rule. It never goes on the logo or on corporate elements such as headers, nav or buttons.

## Seasonality note

The month strips show **when the raw material is harvested and processed**, not when the product is available. Frozen, aseptic and freeze-dried products ship **all year**. In layouts, the strip is drawn as 12 slanted tabs, with the active months filled in the family tint.
