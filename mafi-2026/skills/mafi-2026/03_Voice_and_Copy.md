# 03 · Voice & Copy

## The voice in one line
**A serious industrial supplier that clearly loves food.** It is professional yet approachable: confident, specific and calm. MAFI earns trust with facts (°Brix, kilograms per drum, months at −18 °C, tonnes per year), not adjectives.

**Tone traits** (from the identity manual): Wisdom · Clarity · Inspiration · Knowledgeable · Transparent · Objective · Visionary.

| MAFI sounds like | MAFI never sounds like |
|---|---|
| "Batch after batch." | "Revolutionary!!" |
| "24 months at −18 °C." | "Long-lasting freshness you'll love" |
| "From Egypt to the world." | Consumer-brand cuteness, puns on fruit |
| "Premium, reliable, scalable." | "The cheapest", or any price talk in brand copy |
| Calm pride in Egyptian agriculture | Apology, or exaggeration |

## Lines to use

| Role | Line | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Tagline (the only one)** | **Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation** | Everywhere: covers, end cards, footers, facades |
| Signature form | **MAFI… Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation** | Footer bars, slide footers, social sign-offs |
| Brand essence (positioning) | Guiding the Future of Agri-Food with Knowledge and Innovation. | Strategy decks, about pages. Not a tagline. |
| Corporate line | Driving Sustainability with Purpose, Leading Innovation with Heart. | Company profile, sustainability and corporate pieces |
| Supporting headlines | Reshaping Agri-Food Excellence for a Sustainable Future · Pure, Sustainable & Premium: Agri-Food Products for Global Needs · From Egypt to the world · One system, from field to factory · Batch after batch | Headlines, section openers, screens, film |

Write the tagline in Title Case. In the signature form, use **one ellipsis character** (…) after MAFI. Don't add a second tagline to a piece.

## Person
- **"We" and "our" for MAFI.** "We prioritise locally sourced raw materials…"
- **Product copy is third person**, using "MAFI's" or "Our": "MAFI's IQF Mango captures the full essence of ripe mangoes."
- **"You" appears rarely**, and only in buyer-benefit lines such as "Global Quality You Can Trust" or "Elevate your recipes."

## Casing & naming
- **MAFI** is always in capitals. The descriptor is **FOR AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE INDUSTRIES**, in capitals inside the logo lockup.
- **Product names are ALL CAPS in print headings** (ORANGE JUICE CONCENTRATE) and Title Case in running text (Orange Juice Concentrate).
- **Section openers are caps:** OUR PRODUCTS, CORE VALUES, SUSTAINABILITY, IDEAL FOR.
- **Social headlines are Title Case or Sentence case**: "When Nature Leads, Innovation Follows."
- **Value and benefit labels are ALL CAPS, two short lines:** SUSTAINABLE / BY NATURE · INNOVATIVE / BY DESIGN · GLOBAL QUALITY / YOU CAN TRUST · BUILDING A GREENER / TOMORROW
- **Write IQF, NFC, FCOJ, CPO, CIP and °Brix exactly like this.** Spell out IQF once on long pieces: "IQF (Individually Quick Frozen)".
- **Use "tonnes"** consistently (t/yr in tables). Don't mix with "tons".

## Punctuation & rhythm
- **Two- and three-part cadences:** See · Taste · Smell · Feel. "Sustainably, innovatively, and responsibly."
- **Short display lines end with full stops:** "Crafted from Nature. Shaped by MAFI."
- **Nature-then-MAFI construction** is a signature: "When Nature Leads, Innovation Follows." · "Perfected by Nature.. Delivered by MAFI" · "Crafted from Nature. Shaped by MAFI."
- **Em dashes mark the pivot in corporate prose:** "…the future of the agri-food industry—sustainably, innovatively, and responsibly."
- **Value headings take a colon** in long-form: "Uncompromising Quality:"
- **Headlines split across two or three short lines** rather than running long.
- **Exclamation marks** appear only in event announcements ("We're coming to Gulfood 2026!").
- **No questions as headlines** (one exception: "Why MAFI?" as a section title).

## Emoji, hashtags, symbols
- **Emoji:** functional only. A country flag beside a place in #MAFI_around_the_world posts ("Sunshine for Breakfast 🇪🇸"). Never in headings, print or product copy.
- **Hashtags:** one campaign tag in `#Snake_Case`, set small on the artwork (`#MAFI_around_the_world`).
- **Icons, not Unicode symbols:** no ✓ → ★ as decoration.

## Word bank
**Use:** premium · pure · natural · consistent · reliable · scalable · export-grade · traceable · field to factory · harvest · crafted · preserved · peak ripeness · integrated · precision · clean-label · Egypt's fertile lands · global markets · partnership · ingredient

**Avoid:**
- revolutionary · game-changing · disruptive (exception: "revolutionizing production technology" as a screen headline in the facility)
- cheap · cheapest · discount · best price
- "world's best", or any unprovable superlative
- invented figures

## Bilingual & Arabic
- The Arabic name is **لتصنيع الحاصلات الزراعية**. Use the official Arabic or bilingual logo files only. Never typeset the Arabic lockup yourself.
- **Arabic typeface:** GE SS (headings bold, 30–100 pt; body regular, 12 pt).
- **Have a native reader check all Arabic copy.** AI-generated Arabic is frequently garbled.

## The brand narrative (corporate film, Sept 2026, cleaned)
Use this as the reference for long-form tone: pacing, sentence length and the arc from purpose to place, scale, process, people and sustainability.

> Every generation inherits a challenge. Ours is to nourish a world that demands more quality, more consistency, more responsibility. Because the future of food will be defined not only by what we produce, but by how intelligently, efficiently and responsibly we produce it.
>
> That story begins close to the source, at the heart of Egypt. In Sadat City, MAFI stands close to fertile agricultural lands and connected to the gateways carrying Egyptian quality to the world. Here, agricultural potential meets industrial capability.
>
> This is MAFI: a 157,000-square-metre integrated industrial complex, bringing together five specialised factories and 23 production and packaging lines, with a total planned annual production capacity exceeding 100,000 tonnes. One ecosystem connecting concentrates, cloudy concentrates, IQF and freeze-dry, with receiving laboratories, utilities, storage, packaging and dispatch.
>
> Every product journey begins with the harvest. Fresh raw materials arrive from the field and enter a controlled production environment, carrying the quality of the harvest into every stage that follows. They are transformed into ingredients made for the world, protecting consistency, quality and the character of the harvest. Batch after batch.
>
> Transformation at this scale needs power. Five state-of-the-art boilers generate a combined 60 tonnes of steam per hour. Heat recovery makes better use of energy. Our closed-loop cooling keeps water in circulation, while our ammonia refrigeration plant maintains the temperature each process demands.
>
> Because quality isn't only created, it's protected. Advanced CIP systems keep every line clean and ready for what comes next.
>
> Behind it all is a connected world of technology. Automation brings speed and precision. Digital intelligence brings visibility and control. And as AI becomes part of this ecosystem, data becomes insight, helping us move smarter, respond faster and continuously improve.
>
> Different technology. Different applications. One manufacturing platform, built for leading food manufacturers across global markets. Eight large refrigerated storage rooms hold up to 45,000 pallets and 50,000 tonnes of product, while eight outbound reefer container gates support the cold chain. From Sadat City to customers around the world.
>
> Behind every shipment is a wider commitment: internationally recognised standards, world-class technical expertise, responsible local sourcing, water efficiency, by-product repurposing, recyclable packaging and a planned six-megawatt solar power system.
>
> Because efficiency, food safety, quality and sustainability are not separate ambitions. They are one system: from field to factory, from engineering to ingredient, from Egypt to the world.
>
> **MAFI… Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation.**

## Short boilerplates
**25 words:** MAFI is an Egyptian–Emirati producer of premium B2B fruit and vegetable ingredients: concentrates, purées, IQF and freeze-dried, made in Sadat City for global food manufacturers.

**50 words:** MAFI for Agricultural Produce Industries is a joint Egyptian–Emirati venture making premium B2B agri-food ingredients. Its 157,000 m² complex in Sadat City brings together five factories and 23 lines: citrus and cloudy concentrates, tomato and multi-fruit concentrates and purées, IQF fruits and vegetables, and freeze-dried fruit. It is 100% export-oriented.

**100 words:** add the planned capacity (more than 100,000 tonnes a year), the integrated cold chain (45,000 pallets / 50,000 tonnes), the engineering partners (JBT, GEA, Cabinplant, CFT) and the sustainability programme (LEED Gold design, a planned 6 MW solar system, full traceability). Close with the tagline.
