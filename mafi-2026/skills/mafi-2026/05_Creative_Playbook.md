# 05 · Creative Playbook

This is the part that gives you room. The core in `04_Visual_Identity.md` keeps the work recognisable; this file is about making it **good** and **different each time**.

## What MAFI loves (the recurring ideas behind its best work)

1. **The product *is* the graphic.** This is the strongest idea in MAFI's work, so push it.
   - Countdown numbers built from freeze-dried strawberries and IQF vegetables (posts 19, 20).
   - The year "2025" filled with juices (post 04).
   - "Eid" lettered in purée (post 26).
   - A mosque outline drawn in tomato concentrate above five purée cups (post 10).
   - Easter eggs made of MAFI products (post 29).
   - A fruit-splash Christmas tree (post 24).
   - A moss-topped strawberry for Earth Day (post 32).
2. **Science holding nature.** Lab beakers with measuring marks, filled with juice, purée or concentrate (post 14, post 25, the brochure product pages). It says *precision + natural* in one image.
3. **Liquid in motion.** Essence dripping from a lime slice into a bottle (posts 01, 02), juice pouring over a salad (07), guava purée dripping onto a croissant (11), a tomato splashing into a beaker (14), purée brush-smears (09).
4. **From ingredient to application.**
   - The product sits next to what it becomes, as "IDEAL FOR" photo grids (posts 03, 05).
   - Handwritten labels with curved arrows point at each dish (posts 33, 35).
5. **#MAFI_around_the_world.** Golden-hour scenes from MAFI's markets, each with one dish made with a MAFI ingredient: a Spanish breakfast, a Paris fraisier, a German Käsekuchen (posts 13, 15, 16). Warm, cinematic and a little romantic. Brand-building, not selling.
6. **Overhead abundance.** The big flat-lay of everything MAFI makes, in white bowls and glass (`assets/imagery/hero-portfolio-flatlay.jpg`). Use it for covers and "portfolio" moments.
7. **The green stage.** The deep emerald-to-black exhibition look, with fruit and juice bursting out of darkness (`reference/exhibition-panels-2026/`). Use it for trade shows and statements.
8. **Big honest scale.** Real plant aerials, steel, the site plan with figures, and capacity lower thirds (`assets/film-stills/`). Proof, not decoration.
9. **Calm structure.** Spec panels, seasonality tabs, four-up icon rows, a short rule under every heading. Order makes the food look even better.

## What MAFI doesn't do
- **Visual style:**
  - No cluttered, shouty layouts or neon colours.
  - No consumer-brand cuteness, mascots, cartoon fruit or illustration styles.
  - No black-and-white photos, grain or heavy filters.
- **Brand elements:**
  - No full-page gradient backgrounds, and no gradient behind body text.
  - No new colours, and no third typeface.
- **Content:**
  - No price talk or superlatives.
  - No invented figures.
- **Imagery:** stock photos are never passed off as MAFI's plant. AI-rendered text and logos are never left in a final piece.

## Idea starters by surface (use, remix or ignore)

### Social posts (1:1 or 4:5)
| Type | Recipe |
|---|---|
| **Product hero** | The product in a beaker or bowl, plus one motion element (drip, pour or splash), with the name and a 3-word benefit. Daylight mood. |
| **Applications** | The product at the centre, 3–4 applications around it, handwritten labels with curved arrows. |
| **Product-as-material** | Turn the product into the message: letters, numbers, shapes, a flag, a landmark. Ideal for occasions and countdowns. |
| **Around the world** | A golden-hour scene + dish + flag emoji + `#MAFI_around_the_world`. |
| **Fact card** | One big number in Alverata (157,000 m², 23 lines, 45,000 pallets, 12 freeze-dryers, 60 t/h of steam) on Green Flood or white, with a real plant still. |
| **Event** | Logo + show logo, a headline ("We're coming to …"), and info pills for dates, venue, hall and stand plus the meeting email. Countdown series: 3 → 2 → 1 → "Today", each built from a different product family. |
| **National & religious days** | Respectful, product-led, never a hard sell. Egypt's national days, Eid, Ramadan, Christmas, Easter, Earth Day, World Environment Day. |

### Print (leaflets, brochure pages, spec sheets, profiles)
- **Product leaflet:**
  - A hero product in glass or a bowl, plus a big macro of the fruit cropped off the edge (the brochure's product page).
  - The title in the family colour + black, a two-line description, spec panels, the seasonality strip and a family-coloured edge rule.
- **Family leaflet:**
  - Cover: an opener photo with the family strapline.
  - Inside: a grid of all products in the family with key specs, then an applications strip. Back: contacts.
- **Corporate leaflet:**
  - Cover: the flat-lay inside the sweeping gradient band, or a giant white sprout over a photo.
  - Inside: key figures, the five factories, certifications row, sustainability icons. Back: tagline and contacts.
- **Spec sheet:** the Document mood. White, small confident type, hairline rules, every figure. It doesn't need to be pretty, just impeccable.

### Exhibition & large format
- Green Flood. A huge Alverata headline (80–100 pt equivalent), product splashes, real plant photography, a short list of figures, the stand number and the meeting email.
- Panels read from 5 m away: one idea per panel.

### Presentations (16:9)
**Hold decks to a high bar:** they should be visual first, with photography, products, maps, figures and logos on every slide. Type supports the image.
- Alternate moods across slides (Immersive opener → Daylight content → Green Flood statement → Document data). Never use the same layout twice in a row.
- The footer signature is optional: "MAFI… Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation" with a page number.

### Web & digital
- A white background, generous white space, green primary (headlines, buttons, the leaf as the heading marker), blue secondary (subheads, info, footer), black body text, product cut-outs on white. Buttons darken on hover.
- Catalogue filters: family · product type · application · seasonality.
- Motion: calm fades and reveals, nothing bouncy.

### Video
- Use the corporate film's grammar:
  - golden-hour agriculture, then cool steel interiors
  - macro food in slow motion
  - capacity lower thirds (green + white pills)
  - an end card with logo and tagline
- Subtitles go in a dark rounded box, in a font with a proper capital I.

### Packaging & private label
- The product visual dominates, with the family colour accent, clean Avenir type and **"Produced by MAFI"**.
- Typeset every word for real and proofread it twice.

## Using AI image tools (important)
MAFI's 2026 film showed what goes wrong: AI-generated packs read **"Produced by MAR"**, **"Frexze Drled Mango"** and **"Freshly Frarm Flavor"**, the Arabic signage came out garbled, and the JBT logo became "IBT".

1. **Never let an image model render text, logos, labels or Arabic.** Generate the image clean, then add the real logo file and real type in layout.
2. **AI suits styling and mood:** food scenes, splashes, backgrounds, product-as-material concepts.
3. **Use real photography for capability claims:** the plant, lines, certifications and people. Use `assets/film-stills/` and `assets/imagery/`.
4. **Match MAFI's food look:** bright natural light, soft shadows, appetising colour, glassware and white ceramics, white or light marble surfaces. For the green stage: a dark emerald backdrop with rim light.
5. **Prompt starter:**
   > premium food ingredient photography, [product] in a clear laboratory beaker with measurement marks, [fresh fruit] beside it, a slow-motion drip of [liquid], bright soft daylight, white seamless background, crisp detail, appetising natural colour, minimal, no text, no logos

## Before you ship
- [ ] Can you name the mood (Daylight, Green Flood or Immersive)? Is it different from the previous piece in the series?
- [ ] Logo: the real file, clear space respected, white on photos.
- [ ] The colour hierarchy holds: **white background, green primary, blue secondary, black accent**. Family colours only for product families. Alverata + Avenir only.
- [ ] One leaf idea, used with intent.
- [ ] The food looks delicious and it's the hero.
- [ ] Facts and specs match this pack, and no AI text is left anywhere.
- [ ] It looks like MAFI at thumbnail size: lots of white, green leading, blue supporting, black for text, and glowing food.
