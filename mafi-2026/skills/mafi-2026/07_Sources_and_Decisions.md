## Known issues in the source material (don't repeat them)
- **Film:** AI-generated retail packs read "Produced by MAR", "Frexze Drled Mango" and "Freshly Frarm Flavor". Arabic signage is garbled, the JBT logo reads "IBT", "Prodcasting Center" is misspelled, and the subtitle font makes the capital I look like a lowercase l ("MAFl"). There are also grammar slips in the voice-over subtitles. The narrative in `03_Voice_and_Copy.md` is the corrected version.
- **Film KPIs** shown on AI-generated screens (7,000 kg/h, 98.5% efficiency, 124.8 t/h, Sep 2036 date) are illustrative, not real figures. **Don't quote them.**
- **Profile p18:** one application photo still carries a stock-library watermark.
- **Brochure:** spec panels are set in Kohinoor Bangla (a stand-in font). Use Avenir Next.
