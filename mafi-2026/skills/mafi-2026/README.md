# MAFI Brand Pack: start here

The complete, conflict-free knowledge of **MAFI — for Agricultural Produce Industries**: the company, all 26 products, the voice, the visual identity, the creative habits, and every approved asset. It was built in October 2026 from all MAFI sources (corporate film, brochure, company profile, identity manual, decks, 38 social posts, exhibition panels) and merged by priority. **Where sources disagreed, the decision is already made** (`07_Sources_and_Decisions.md`).

**Two editions with identical content:** `MAFI_Brand_Pack.zip` (full-resolution product images, for designers and print) and `MAFI_Brand_Pack_LLM.zip` (product images downscaled to 800 px, small enough to upload to AI tools).

**If you are an AI model:** read this file fully, then the chapters in order. Treat files 01–05 as the rules and `reference/` as inspiration. Within the fixed core, you are expected to be creative.

## The brief in 60 seconds
- **Who:** MAFI is a joint Egyptian–Emirati maker of **premium B2B fruit and vegetable ingredients**. Its 157,000 m² complex in **Sadat City, Egypt** has **5 factories** and **23 lines**, with planned capacity of **more than 100,000 tonnes a year**. It is **100% export-oriented**, serving North America, Europe, Asia and the GCC.
- **What:** **26 products in 4 families:**
  - Citrus (8)
  - Tomato & Multi-Fruits (5)
  - Frozen Fruits & Vegetables / IQF (12)
  - Freeze Dry (1 product, 4 cuts)
- **Who buys:** F&B manufacturers, processors, retailers and private label, HoReCa. MAFI sells ingredients, **never consumer brands**.
- **Tagline:** ***Pioneering the Future of Food Innovation*** (the only one)
- **Voice:** professional yet approachable. Confident, specific, calm; facts over adjectives. "We" for MAFI. No hype, no price talk.
- **Feeling:** bright, clean and appetising, *a laboratory that smells like an orchard*. The food is the hero and the design stays calm.

### Colour
| Role | Colour | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Background | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Primary | MAFI Green | `#009C49` |
| Secondary | MAFI Blue | `#045976` |
| Accent | Black | `#000000` |

That's the whole palette: **no turquoise or any other brand colour.** Product families have their own tag colours, used only to label products.

### Type, logo and devices
- **Type:** **Alverata** for headings, **Avenir Next** for everything else, **GE SS** for Arabic. Free fallbacks: Faustina / Nunito Sans / Noto Kufi Arabic.
- **Logo:** always the supplied file, with 18% clear space. Never redrawn, and never generated by AI.
- **The leaf and the sprout** are core devices. Use them generously but with intent.

### What MAFI loves
- the product used *as* the graphic (letters, numbers and shapes made of fruit and purée)
- lab beakers
- liquid in motion
- ingredient → application grids with handwritten arrows
- #MAFI_around_the_world golden-hour scenes
- overhead flat-lays
- the deep **Green Flood** stage look for events
- real plant photography as proof
- calm structure: spec panels, month tabs, a short rule under headings

### What MAFI doesn't do
- no clutter, cartoon fruit, or new colours and typefaces
- no gradient behind text
- no invented figures
- no AI-rendered text or logos
- no stock photos passed off as MAFI's plant

## How much freedom you have
- **Fixed:**
  - the logo files
  - the four colours and their roles (plus product-family tags for products only)
  - Alverata + Avenir Next
  - the leaf & sprout shapes
  - facts and figures exactly as given
  - the tagline
- **Strong defaults (lean on them, adapt freely):** family colour coding, spec panels, month tabs, heading + green rule, rounded photos, pills, the green→blue edge.
- **Your call:** composition, scale, density, mood (Daylight, Green Flood or Immersive), crop, where the logo sits, whether the gradient appears at all. **Make each piece in a series different from the last.**
